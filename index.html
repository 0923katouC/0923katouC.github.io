THEORETICAL PHYSICS · GRAVITATION · COSMOLOGY

CMC

PhD student in theoretical physics, working on gravitational-wave theory, dark matter, compact objects, and related problems in relativistic astrophysics.

Research Publications

Research interests

  • Gravitational-wave theory
  • Pulsar timing arrays and supermassive black-hole binaries
  • Dark-matter phenomenology
  • Extreme mass-ratio inspirals and compact objects

Current focus

Methods for gravitational-wave source modeling, inference, and environmental effects across nanoHertz to milliHertz frequency bands.

Selected topics

Pulsar timing arrays

Finite-pulsar effects, harmonic-space methods, stochastic backgrounds, and individually resolvable sources.

Dark matter

Dark-matter environments around compact objects and their imprint on gravitational-wave dynamics.

Compact binaries

Orbital dynamics, radiation, environmental interactions, and beyond-vacuum signatures.
