Welcome

A short note marking the first version of this academic website.

The site is intentionally lightweight: plain HTML, CSS, a very small JavaScript file, and MathJax for mathematical notation. This keeps deployment transparent and makes future migration to a static-site generator straightforward.

Notation

For gravitational-wave discussions, the observed frequency at Earth is denoted by $f_{\rm gw}$, while the source-frame gravitational-wave frequency is $f_s$. A cosmological redshift $z$ gives

\[f_{\rm gw}=\frac{f_s}{1+z}.\]

Future research notes can use the same MathJax setup for equations and derivations.
