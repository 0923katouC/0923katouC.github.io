Research

Research themes in gravitational-wave theory, dark matter, compact objects, and relativistic astrophysics.

Pulsar timing arrays

I am interested in gravitational-wave signals in the nanoHertz band, including stochastic backgrounds and individually resolvable supermassive black-hole binaries. Current methodological interests include harmonic-space descriptions, finite-pulsar effects, detector anisotropy, and source-detection statistics.

Dark matter and compact objects

I study how non-vacuum environments and additional fields can affect compact-object dynamics and gravitational radiation, with particular interest in dark-matter distributions, bosonic fields, and environmental signatures.

Extreme mass-ratio inspirals

Another direction is the modeling of EMRIs in non-vacuum environments, including orbital evolution, additional fluxes, and detector-response effects relevant to space-based gravitational-wave observations.
